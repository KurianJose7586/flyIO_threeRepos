Jabalpur Tourism

Jabalpur is a popular weekend getaway in Madhya Pradesh, visited mainly for the Narmada gorge at Bhedaghat and as a gateway to Kanha and Bandhavgarh tiger reserves.

Top Places to Visit

Madan Mahal Fort

An 11th-century Gond fort on a granite hill, with views over the city. Allow an hour; the climb is short but steep.

Balancing Rock

A boulder resting on another at a single point, said to have survived earthquakes. Free entry, near the Madan Mahal road.

Best Time to Visit

October to March, when days are 15–28°C. Avoid May and June.

How to Reach

Jabalpur Junction connects to most major cities by train. Kanha National Park is about 160 km away by road — around four hours.
