Jabalpur

Jabalpur is a city in Madhya Pradesh, central India, best known for the marble rocks at Bhedaghat and the Dhuandhar Falls on the Narmada river.

Understand

Jabalpur sits on the Narmada river in the Mahakoshal region. It served as the capital of the Gond kingdom in the 12th century and later became a major cantonment town under British administration.

Climate

Summers from April to June are hot, reaching 45°C. The monsoon runs from July to September. Winters are mild and are the most comfortable time to visit.

Get in

By plane

Jabalpur Airport (JLR) at Dumna is about 20 km from the city centre, with direct flights to Delhi, Mumbai, Hyderabad and Indore.

By train

Jabalpur Junction is a major stop on the Howrah–Mumbai line and the headquarters of the West Central Railway. Direct trains connect to Delhi, Mumbai, Chennai, Kolkata and Bhopal.

See

Bhedaghat Marble Rocks

Towering soapstone and dolomite cliffs rise up to 30 m on either side of the Narmada for about 3 km. Boat rides run from the ghat; the stretch is especially striking under a full moon.

Dhuandhar Falls

The Narmada plunges 30 m here, throwing up enough spray to earn the name "smoke cascade". A cable car crosses the gorge for an overhead view.

Sleep

Budget and mid-range hotels cluster around the railway station and Napier Town. MPTDC operates a rest house near Bhedaghat.
